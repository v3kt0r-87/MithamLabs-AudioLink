Privacy Policy

Audiolink by MithamLabs
← Back

Audiolink by MithamLabs is built on a zero-knowledge peer-to-peer (P2P) architecture. We do not inspect, log, harvest, or monetize your communications.

1. Direct Peer-to-Peer Media

Voice audio streams directly between browser endpoints using WebRTC. Streams are end-to-end encrypted using DTLS-SRTP standards. No media ever routes through or is stored on MithamLabs servers.

2. Ephemeral Signaling

To connect calls, temporary handshake metadata (usernames, session descriptions, and network candidates) is briefly exchanged via the PeerJS signaling network. This signaling channel does not store conversation content or logs.

3. Device Hardware & Permissions

Microphone access is requested strictly to capture and stream outgoing voice during active calls. Hardware processing (echo cancellation and noise suppression) executes entirely locally inside your browser.

4. Zero Tracking & Local Storage

There are no tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or user profiling. Browser localStorage is used solely to remember your chosen username and UI theme preference.

5. Authors & Maintainers

Audiolink is created and maintained under MithamLabs by Shankar Vallabhan (@v3kt0r-87) and Priyanshu Pandey (@LordShenron).
